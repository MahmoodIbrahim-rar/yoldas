# Google Ads Preparation

The welcome page includes two decorative, responsive ad-ready rails only on wide desktop layouts. They are deliberately empty and have no network request, Google script, publisher identifier, tracking tag, or advertisement content.

| Slot identifier | Intended position | Current behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `welcome-left-rail` | Left of the welcome composition on wide screens | Shows a labelled placeholder only. |
| `welcome-right-rail` | Right of the welcome composition on wide screens | Shows a labelled placeholder only. |

Before enabling Google AdSense, the site owner must add only the official code issued for the approved publisher account, configure the associated consent and privacy disclosures, and test with real policy-compliant ads. Do not use invented ad copy, placements, or publisher identifiers.

## Local layout verification

The 1600 × 900 local preview was checked on 2026-08-25. Both rails remained visible, the orange hero and introductory card stayed readable, and no advertisement content or third-party advertising script was loaded.
